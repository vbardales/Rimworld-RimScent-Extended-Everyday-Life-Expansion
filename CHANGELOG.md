# 0.1.0

- Initial standalone repository for the RimWorld 1.6 release.
- Adds scent definitions and conditional integrations for everyday cooking, flowers,
  household items, coffee, baking, Stoneborn Cuisine, Alpha Crafts, Knick Knacks,
  Colonists' Deco, and Tabletop Trove.
- Creates `Mod/About/PublishedFileId.txt` on first Workshop publication; its actual ID must be
  committed immediately and must never be invented.
